# Code of Conduct / آیین رفتار

PinHarbor welcomes respectful participation. Be constructive, patient with newcomers and precise in bug reports. Critique designs and code, not people. Discrimination, harassment, doxxing, threats and publishing another person's credentials are not acceptable.

If you encounter harmful behavior, report it privately to the repository maintainers through the repository's contact/security channel; do not publish sensitive details in a public Issue. Maintainers may edit, lock or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary.

در گفتگوها محترمانه رفتار کنید؛ از توهین، آزار، انتشار اطلاعات خصوصی و تبعیض خودداری کنید. نقد فنی باید متوجه محصول و کد باشد، نه افراد.
